# Bundled typefaces

Both faces are the ones used on hhgoa.com, self-hosted here so canvas rendering never
waits on a third-party CDN. Both are licensed under the
[SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org/), which permits bundling and
redistribution.

Files here are the Latin and Latin-Extended `woff2` subsets served by Google Fonts.
Static TTF instances used for server-side OG image rendering live in `src/assets/`.
